# Catalog photos

For the main photo of each product, use a square frame. Center the entire product and keep its original proportions. The larger dimension of the product should occupy about 90% of the frame, leaving about 5% on each side along that axis. Check the result visually before publishing.

Use separate, verified photos of the exact model for additional gallery views. Keep the original framing of intentional detail shots and avoid making additional gallery images by cropping the main photo.
